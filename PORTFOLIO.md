**Zarra**

Small-model adaptation experiments · Raza Labs by Ali Raza

Category: Small-model adaptation

Stage: Independent prototype

Role: Product design and development, with AI-assisted implementation

A reproducible local BERT/PEFT intent-classification experiment comparing a rule baseline, an unadapted classifier, and a LoRA adapter on a synthetic held-out split.

Verification: Actual local LoRA training and inference.

Repository: https://github.com/aliraza2025/intake-model-lab

Demo: https://aliraza2025.github.io/intake-model-lab/

Scope qualification: The experiment uses 30 synthetic training examples and 12 synthetic held-out examples. Both rules and the adapter scored 100% on this tiny set. This establishes no benefit over rules and no customer generalization. The unadapted classifier head is newly initialized.
